Homepage headline · For discussion

Does “Moving you forward.” do enough work?

The headline is the first thing a general contractor, owner or job seeker reads. In about five seconds, it should tell them what MPC does, where, and for whom.

Right now “Moving you forward.” is the only text in the hero. Below are three options to consider, our recommendation, and a few questions that will help us settle it together.

The current headline

Why we think it needs support

It’s a positive line with real energy, and the idea of momentum is worth keeping. On its own, though, it leaves the visitor to work out the rest.

  • Any company could say it

    A paving firm, a logistics company or a car dealer could use the same words. Nothing in the line says concrete, commercial work or New England.

  • “You” isn’t clear

    The homepage speaks to two groups: general contractors and owners sending RFPs, and people looking for work. The line doesn’t clearly speak to either.

  • It makes no promise

    Forward how? Faster schedules, better slabs, room to grow? A strong headline answers one question the visitor already has.

  • Search engines can’t read it

    Google uses the main heading to work out what a page is about. This one never mentions concrete, commercial work or New Hampshire.

Our recommendation

Option B, and keep “Moving you forward.” elsewhere

“Concrete that keeps your project moving.” keeps the spirit of your line and answers the visitor’s first question. Adding the supporting line makes the hero complete for general contractors and for search engines.

If “Moving you forward.” matters to you as a brand line, it works well as a closing statement: at the end of the homepage, next to the RFP button, or in the footer. By then the visitor knows who you are.

Help us decide

Four questions for MPC

  1. Who do you most need to reach on the homepage?

    General contractors sending RFPs, or people applying for jobs? The headline should speak to the main group. The second button can serve the other.

  2. What do general contractors actually pick you for?

    Schedule, slab quality, being easy to work with, something else? Whatever they say is the most honest version of “forward”.

  3. What does “Moving you forward” mean to you?

    If it’s about growth or how you treat your people, it may land harder on Who We Are or Careers than in the hero.

  4. Can we add one short line under the headline?

    We took it out after our call. If you’d still rather not have it, the headline has to say everything by itself, which points to Option B or C.

A quick way to check

The five-second test

Show the homepage to someone who doesn’t know MPC for five seconds, then ask them three questions. A strong hero lets them answer all three.

  • What does this company do?
  • Where do they work?
  • Who are they for?
